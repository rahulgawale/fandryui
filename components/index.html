Avatar

Overlays & Data

Command

A command palette — a modal search box over a list of actions. Generic: it reports the chosen value and leaves what it does (and the Cmd+K/Ctrl+K shortcut) to you.

Overlays & Data

Dialog

A modal panel over a backdrop, with Escape/backdrop-click to close and focus returned to the trigger.

Overlays & Data

Menu

Overlays & Data

Menu Item

A single selectable row inside fandry-menu.

Overlays & Data

Popover

An anchored floating panel — owns positioning, not the trigger or content. The panel is never narrower than its trigger; set display: block on the popover for a trigger that fills its row.

Overlays & Data

Table

A data table with sorting, pagination, selection, and filtering — wraps @tanstack/table-core.

Salesforce

Lookup

Find and pick a record — one by default, or several with multiple. It fetches nothing itself: it reports what was typed, you run the query and hand the records back.
